How commenting builds presence
  • Commenting places Rituraj’s name in front of the post author’s full audience — often hundreds or thousands of senior professionals in the target sector who would not otherwise encounter his profile.
  • Consistent presence signals credibility. A LinkedIn profile that appears regularly in high-quality discussions is treated as an active expert — making connection requests and future content more likely to be noticed and accepted.
  • Volume scaling is intentional. Starting at 4 and building to 10 per session allows early-month comments to settle and generate engagement before increasing frequency — reducing algorithm flag risk and building a natural activity curve.
